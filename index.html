The apartment

Built to live in.
Fitted to work from.

MZV3 is a 60m² one-bedroom apartment on the third floor of Magnolieholm — a 2019 new-build facing Sluseholmen's canal network. The building is quiet, well-run, and two minutes from the waterfront.

The flat has been furnished from scratch for professionals arriving with a suitcase. The bedroom doubles as a proper workspace: a 131cm desk with drawers, an ergonomic chair, and a 32" Samsung monitor on an articulating arm that swings between desk and bed-viewing position. One USB-C cable connects your laptop to screen, power, and audio.

A 50" smart TV is wall-mounted in the living room. Both screens stream natively — no separate stick needed. The kitchen is fully equipped. The building has fibre.

Sage-painted walls, oak floors, VELFAC triple-glazed windows. Quiet enough to focus. Connected enough to arrive on day one and get to work.

Location

Sluseholmen —
the city's quiet canal island

Magnolieholm sits at the northern edge of Sluseholmen, the canal district that became Copenhagen's most architecturally considered neighbourhood of the 2010s. Brick facades, waterways, and a human scale that the rest of the city doesn't quite manage.

The metro runs you to central Copenhagen in under 20 minutes. Fisketorvet shopping and the harbour bath are a short cycle away. The neighbourhood has what you need without being in the middle of everything.

  • Sluseholmen Metro (M4) 1 min walk
  • Copenhagen Central Station 12 min by metro
  • Copenhagen Airport 25 min by metro
  • Fisketorvet (shopping) 5 min metro / 8 min cycle
  • Vesterbro / city centre 15 min cycle
  • Supermarkets (365discount, Netto, Spar) 1–3 min walk
  • Restaurants & cafés from 1 min walk
